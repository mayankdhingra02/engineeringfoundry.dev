# Content policy

This document is an internal operating rule for Engineering Foundry content. It is not legal advice.

## Third-party material

- Link to third-party problem pages rather than reproducing their statements.
- Store only discovery metadata: title, source, public URL, publicly known difficulty, high-level taxonomy, and original Engineering Foundry notes.
- Do not redistribute premium, paywalled, proprietary, private, or scraped question banks.
- Do not copy course explanations, editorial solutions, acceptance rates, interview-frequency claims, or employer-tag datasets.
- Do not automate access to LeetCode, Glassdoor, Blind, Educative, NeetCode, or similar services.
- External links must use HTTPS, identify the destination, open safely, and be covered by content validation.

## Original material

Engineering Foundry may publish complete prompts and explanations that it authors. Original records must use the `original` source platform, contain no required external URL, and carry no invented company association.

## Attribution and provenance

- Every external question, resource, company association, and company-specific claim needs a source record.
- A source record identifies its name, platform, URL, verification state, and an actual last-verified date when one exists.
- A current link does not prove a company association or process claim. Those assertions require their own provenance.
- Unavailable links remain in content history with a non-active status and are excluded from public listings.

## Community submissions

Community-reported material must remain labeled as such until independently reviewed. A later submission workflow must include moderation, attribution review, privacy safeguards, and a way to correct or remove outdated material.
