# Engineering Foundry v1 Launch Finish Plan

Status: approved repository synthesis

Reviewed: 2026-09-05

Applies to: EF-OPS

## Decision

The repository-owned Required scope is complete when every requirement-family record whose priority is `required` is implemented, the registry contains no unmodeled Required item, generated governance is bound to the exact evaluated commit, and the full local and hosted release lanes pass. Production deployment and provider decisions are not repository completion claims; they remain isolated in `EF-OPS-EXTERNAL-PRODUCTION-GATES`.

## Inputs used

- The authoritative master specification and its explicit stop conditions.
- The machine-readable requirement, research-artifact, and source registries.
- The deterministic content manifest, source ledger, and coverage report.
- The merged Required-family pull-request sequence and its independent focused reviews.
- The repository's static, database, production, dependency, TypeScript, and CodeQL release lanes.

No missing historical planning document is needed to change product scope. This repository synthesis records the plan actually executed and the evidence required to keep the closure truthful.

## Required closure sequence

1. Accept the integrated main branch as the completed cross-route hardening baseline; do not reopen generalized auditing without a failing test or a concrete high-severity defect.
2. Complete one user-facing Required family at a time: DSA, Behavioral, Interview Playbook, Mock Lab, Company Guides, Interview Experiences, Low-Level Design, and Salary Negotiation. Keep already-complete System Design and ML Design closed.
3. For each family, publish missing content and behavior, run focused qualification, obtain an independent acceptance review, run the complete release lane, and merge only after hosted checks pass.
4. Reconcile Supporting Routes using its actual acceptance criteria. Keep employer-specific referral policy as the separately classified P2 deferral, and verify URL/history, private-draft, and guarded-focus behavior in the browser.
5. Close Global Platform from the accepted integrated baseline and close release governance from deterministic generated evidence. Do not convert production, legal, provider, account-enablement, analytics-consent, or founder decisions into repository work.

## Acceptance evidence

- Registries generate byte-deterministic, SHA-bound outputs and reject stale or hand-edited snapshots.
- Canonical date, URL, source, artifact, impact-ledger, and release-record validation remains fail-closed.
- Required user-facing families have explicit routes, implementation paths, tests, acceptance criteria, and zero Required gaps.
- Referrals preserve local drafts through mode history, exclude draft content from URLs and analytics, and restore focus to the selected persistent mode control only when navigation hides the previously focused panel and no other focus claim exists.
- The final coverage report must show no Required blocker and no explicitly unmodeled atomic requirement.
- Deferred P1/P2, founder-approval, new-research, and external-owner records remain visible with their real disposition.

## Deliberate exclusions and external gates

- No production deployment, DNS/TLS claim, hosted migration claim, backup claim, provider configuration, account enablement, analytics consent, or legal approval is inferred.
- AI Basics remains founder-approval work; Visualization Lab remains P1; additional DSA languages and employer-specific referral policy retain their recorded non-Required dispositions.
- Missing historical System Design research inputs remain explicitly recorded even though the separately reviewed published crosswalk closes the Required System Design implementation scope.

## Ongoing rule

Future work does not reopen a completed Required family merely because an optional idea exists. Reopen it only for a failed acceptance test, a concrete blocker, or an approved product decision that changes Required scope.
