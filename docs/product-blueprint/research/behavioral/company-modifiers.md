# Behavioral company-modifier evidence specification

## Approved use

A company modifier can alter story-category emphasis or likely follow-up families only when the supporting claim is current and attributable. It cannot create a duplicate question bank, rewrite canonical facts, imply a hidden scoring policy, or turn public values into keyword stuffing.

Every substantive modifier must record company, verification date, source class, role/level/region/program applicability, behavioral placement, competency emphasis, story-category mapping, follow-up emphasis, confidence, uncertainty, and sources. Source classes remain explicit: current official values or interview guidance; labeled recruiter/interviewer commentary; dated candidate report; or Engineering Foundry preparation inference.

`SRC-BEH-AMAZON-INTERVIEW` is used only as a first-party example that one company publicly describes behavioral questions and STAR preparation. It does not support universal process, exact-question, frequency, hidden-policy, or outcome claims. Other companies require their own claim-level evidence before substantive modifiers publish.

## Exclusions

- no rumored question presented as official;
- no universal process claim from one role, region, or report;
- no instruction to distort a story to resemble a company value;
- no company association added to the canonical 48-question audit without a source ID; and
- no transfer of company claims between hiring programs or dates without reverification.

## Cross-family boundary

This artifact approves the Behavioral modifier evidence model and the current zero-invented-reference behavior. It does not close `EF-COMP` or `EF-COMP-GUIDE-COVERAGE`; company-guide breadth and each published claim remain independently tracked and must be completed in that Required family.
