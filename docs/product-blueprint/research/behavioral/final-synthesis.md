# Behavioral Required implementation synthesis

## Closed product contract

- The original 48-question catalog remains intact and gains audited metadata rather than replacement prompts.
- Sixteen substantive lessons publish as one evidence-first curriculum with exercises, examples, source boundaries, and next actions.
- Canonical private story facts drive concise/standard variants and follow-up preparation; consistency checks reject unsupported additions or contradictions.
- Coverage and the eleven-dimension rubric are descriptive, never readiness scores.
- STAR, CAR, SAR, SOAR, and a natural evidence arc are supported as optional organizers, not evaluators.
- The adaptive text drill chooses a highest-value evidence gap, supports one deeper probe, accepts unknown/unshareable boundaries, and ends with one to three actions plus an Interview Playbook return.
- Entry, Mid / SDE II, Senior, Staff+, International, Small-company, and Individual-contributor contexts are explicit without increasing verbosity by level.
- Public practice stores only safe URL labels. Private story/answer content stays owner-scoped and excluded from analytics.
- The UI excludes unsupported behavioral and hiring inference.
- Direct links and browser Back/Forward restore canonical question, filter, and practice state.

## Evidence reconciliation

The six Behavioral synthesis artifacts were reconciled on September 5, 2026 with `data/behavioral`, the public learning/practice/review routes, the existing private workspace, the deterministic URL-state engine, source disclosures, sitemap/search inventory, regression tests, and desktop/mobile rendered review.

Official source records support structured, job-related evidence, consistent probing, descriptive anchors, role-relative context, and a bounded first-party company example. They do not establish a universal interview process, predict outcomes, or authorize personality/culture-fit inference.
