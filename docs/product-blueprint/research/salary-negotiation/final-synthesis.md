# Salary Negotiation: research-backed section blueprint

Reviewed: 2026-09-05

## Decision

Publish the existing eight-module Salary Negotiation family as a bounded post-offer learning and communication tool. Keep the worksheet in browser memory, keep its arithmetic mechanical, and do not create salary bands, an offer score, equity forecasts, recruiter automation, or individualized legal, tax, securities, immigration, employment, or financial advice.

## Current source synthesis

The U.S. Department of Labor’s 2026 Salary Negotiation Participant Guide supports a whole-offer review, preparation of a supported request, asking for time, and confirming final terms in writing. It does not establish employer policy or guarantee that a term can move. The Department’s compensation-benefits overview reinforces that many benefits depend on the actual employment agreement.

The IRS stock-options topic is the authoritative federal starting point for the limited claim that option tax treatment varies by instrument and by events such as grant, exercise, and disposition. Engineering Foundry therefore does not calculate tax or convert a private grant into predicted cash value.

The FTC’s current Noncompete Rule page states that the federal rule is not in effect and is not enforceable. That status is volatile and does not resolve state law or an individual agreement, so it receives a three-month review window and a qualified-review boundary.

USCIS’s current Form I-765 instructions demonstrate that U.S. work authorization depends on category and documentation. The product must not infer a candidate’s authorization, portability, filing requirement, or deadline from an offer or location field.

## Content and product contract

- Keep the eight exact modules in the master specification and cross-link them.
- Separate guaranteed cash, target bonus, user-entered annualized equity, and subjective factors.
- Use editable, truthful scripts and never generate fake leverage or auto-send a message.
- Keep every worksheet value out of URLs, storage, cookies, server actions, logs, and analytics.
- On clipboard failure, expose and focus a selectable local draft with an accessible status.
- Show source jurisdiction, verification date, review-by date, volatility, claim, and usage limit wherever current legal or tax context is surfaced.
- Treat sources as bounded evidence, not universal instructions; the actual written offer and qualified local review control material decisions.

## Freshness operations

Re-check volatile FTC and USCIS records by 2026-12-05. Re-check periodic DOL and IRS guidance by 2027-03-05. A missed review date makes the affected current-status statement stale; it must be removed or visibly marked unavailable until reverified. Static educational negotiation principles may remain, but cannot silently inherit current legal status from an expired source.
