# DSA competitive product and practice synthesis

## Scope and evidence boundary

This artifact replaces the missing historical input with a repository-verifiable synthesis for the Required DSA family. It compares public product mechanics, not proprietary question corpora or unverifiable outcome claims. Product pages were reviewed on September 5, 2026; volatile marketing claims are not used as Engineering Foundry acceptance evidence.

## Observed product mechanics

- `SRC-DSA-LEETCODE-EXPLORE` documents a topic-card learning flow and automatic completion state. It supports the need for navigable learning structure, but completion remains activity rather than mastery.
- `SRC-DSA-ALGOMONSTER-ROADMAP` exposes prerequisite-ordered foundations and pattern modules, quizzes, focused problems, and company-oriented follow-ons. Engineering Foundry adopts prerequisite order and pattern transfer, not the source's marketing or outcome claims.
- `SRC-DSA-TECH-HANDBOOK-PLAN` organizes study by available time and priority. Engineering Foundry adopts editable time horizons and breadth/depth sequencing, not its universal time recommendation.

## Approved Engineering Foundry decisions

1. Keep one finite, versioned Foundry 75 instead of competing on corpus size.
2. Teach the invariant and decision boundary before an implementation template.
3. Pair every pattern lesson with labeled, unlabeled, and transfer practice.
4. Separate Learn, Recognition, Untimed, Timed, Mixed, and Review modes because they produce different evidence.
5. Hide taxonomy before recognition/timed/mixed selection and disclose it only in an explicit debrief.
6. Treat solved counts, completion marks, and self-ratings as activity or self-report—not observed mastery.
7. Preserve a useful anonymous path and make durable notes, attempts, and review history private to the signed-in owner.
8. Keep company associations outside the canonical problem identity and label demonstration data rather than implying interview frequency.
9. Return attempt evidence to the Interview Playbook with practice mode, prior exposure, and self-report provenance.
10. Prefer deterministic text/HTML traces with accessible explanations; no untrusted code execution is required.

## Acceptance crosswalk

- The 20-pattern index, 20 topic lessons, Foundry 75, six practice modes, and 12 descriptive rubric dimensions are executable repository contracts.
- Public question metadata links to external prompts without reproducing statements or editorials; Engineering Foundry originals are explicitly marked.
- Private attempts use owner-scoped storage, revision checks, export/deletion coverage, and analytics exclusion.
- Browser URL state, exact pattern counts, provenance, routes, and Playbook handoffs are covered by DSA qualification commands.

P1 JavaScript/TypeScript and P2 curriculum shells remain separate tracked requirements. Their incompleteness does not invalidate the Required DSA product already published.
