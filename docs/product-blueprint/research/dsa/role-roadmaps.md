# DSA role-roadmap synthesis

## Boundary

The roadmap changes preparation emphasis; it does not claim a universal hiring bar, predict readiness, or replace a recruiter's current instructions. `SRC-DSA-TECH-HANDBOOK-PLAN` supports time-aware topic prioritization as a product pattern. The exact Engineering Foundry role overlays are editorial decisions tested against the shared canonical catalog.

## Shared progression

All roles retain the same prerequisite spine: complexity and arrays/strings; hashing and range patterns; stacks, queues, search, and linked structures; trees, traversal, and heaps; intervals, backtracking, tries, and connectivity; graphs; then greedy and dynamic programming. A role overlay changes priority, depth, and follow-up behavior—not the meaning of an algorithm.

## SDE I

- Establish reliable implementation of core structures and common Easy/Medium patterns.
- State an invariant, complexity, and normal/boundary tests without excessive narration.
- Use advanced structures only when the target process supplies evidence that they matter.

## SDE II

- Add breadth across graphs, intervals, heaps, greedy, and dynamic programming.
- Practice optimization from a correct baseline and adapt when constraints change.
- Include timed and mixed transfer work after language and core-pattern fluency.

## SDE III+

- Retain coding fluency while increasing ambiguity, API/state design, failure handling, and review depth.
- Explain trade-offs, testability, invariants across multiple structures, and follow-up extensions.
- Treat optional advanced structures as role/company-dependent rather than a universal gate.

## Time horizons

Thirty, sixty, and ninety-day plans are editable schedules. Shorter windows reduce breadth and protect high-priority retrieval; longer windows add interleaving, transfer, and spaced review. Missed days reprioritize future work rather than doubling a backlog. The no-deadline mode remains sustainable and avoids invented urgency.

## Company overlays

Company overlays remain distinct from the transferable roadmap. Official guidance, candidate reports, community observations, EF recommendations, and user-confirmed private details must remain visibly separate. No overlay may introduce a fake frequency, confidential prompt, or universal team/level claim.

## Repository evidence

The level-roadmap and planning qualifications enforce separate SDE I/SDE II/SDE III+ assignments, valid prerequisites, canonical problem reuse, optional/stretch semantics, deterministic company overlays, and the absence of a solved-count readiness score.
