# Final preparation, disruption, and debrief

## Phase behavior

The final week narrows broad learning to verified loop facts, material gap repair, representative rehearsal, and logistics. Three days out freezes broad expansion. The day before uses familiar compact review and closes logistics. Interview day protects execution. Within the final hour the product stops studying, opens only permitted materials, and surfaces the next round, environment, and contact path.

Between rounds, the product verifies the next round and captures only urgent factual notes. It does not replay or score the prior round. Technical failures, schedule conflicts, illness or emergency, unexpected rounds, hostile interactions, and tool-rule conflicts route to bounded factual responses and the appropriate contact path.

`SRC-PLAY-EEOC-ACCOMMODATION` supports U.S.-specific accommodation routing without legal adjudication. `SRC-PLAY-YALE-AFTER-INTERVIEW` supports prompt factual notes and retaining the employer-stated timeline without creating a universal follow-up rule.

## Private debrief

Facts, interpretation, and learning are captured separately. Notes remain category-level, omit proprietary prompt reconstruction, and do not publish automatically or enter analytics. Advancement reprioritizes confirmed rounds; rejection preserves transferable evidence without inventing cause; offer transitions to Salary Negotiation; withdrawal archives the process while retaining reusable learning.

Follow-up uses recruiter-promised timing, then current official instruction, then application context, then an optional general convention. Templates remain editable and are never sent automatically.
