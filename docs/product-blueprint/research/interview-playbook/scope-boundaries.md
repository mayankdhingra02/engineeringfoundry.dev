# Interview Playbook scope and boundaries

## Decision

The Playbook is the interview-preparation control plane. It owns context, uncertainty, prioritization, sequencing, cross-specialist handoffs, final-preparation state, round execution, private debrief, and outcome transitions. It does not duplicate DSA, System Design, ML Design, Low-Level Design, Behavioral, Company Guides, Mock Interviews, Applications, Calendar, or Salary Negotiation.

The public `/interview-tips` surface teaches reusable execution and lifecycle behavior. The signed-in `/interview-playbook` surface applies private applications, rounds, saved constraints, and evidence to one defensible next action. Public checklists remain session-only. Private reflection stays with the owning round.

## Information architecture

- Start: one dominant action for a scheduled, uncertain, overdue, pre-interview, closed, or first-use state.
- Context and diagnostic: progressive inputs with explicit unknowns and user overrides.
- Plan: plan progress, evidence coverage, and preparation phase remain separate.
- Round execution: signal-based quick references and deeper dossiers.
- Final stretch: final week, day before, interview day, and between-round behavior.
- Debrief and transition: private facts, interpretation, learning, and specialist handoff.
- Methodology: reader-facing evidence hierarchy, review dates, claim groups, and limits.

## Permanent exclusions

No readiness percentage, hiring prediction, hidden rubric, proprietary prompt collection, covert live assistance, universal company loop, medical prescription, jurisdiction-free legal conclusion, or automatic public debrief belongs in this family.
