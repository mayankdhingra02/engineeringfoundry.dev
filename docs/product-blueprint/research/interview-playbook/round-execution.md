# Round-execution research and editorial specification

## Method

The taxonomy separates stage, modality, and evaluated signal so labels such as screen, onsite, or final do not silently imply a competency. Sixteen public signal/format guides publish; ten have full dossiers, including Technical Presentation.

`SRC-BEH-OPM-STRUCTURED` and `SRC-BEH-OPM-GUIDE` support the job-related competency and observable-evidence boundary. `SRC-COMP-MICROSOFT-OFFICIAL` and `SRC-COMP-AMAZON-OFFICIAL` are employer-scoped examples of technical preparation, not universal process rules. `SRC-PLAY-EEOC-ACCOMMODATION` supports U.S.-specific routing to an employer accommodation channel. `SRC-PLAY-MIT-PRESENTATION` anchors audience, purpose, message, evidence, and structure for the presentation dossier.

## Dossier contract

Each full dossier exposes intended evaluation, variation, setup, opening, execution flow, adaptable timing, communication checkpoints, hints and redirection, recovery, short-on-time and partial-completion behavior, validation, closing, specialist links, level overlays, environment and accessibility guidance, company/integrity boundaries, post-round questions, reviewed date, source method, and claim groups.

Recovery, seniority overlays, and annotated dialogues are explicitly labeled Engineering Foundry editorial synthesis. They are not employer rubrics, research findings, or transcripts.

## Technical presentation

The published guide covers audience and assignment clarification, a central conclusion, personal ownership, constraints and alternatives, evidence and limitations, accessible visuals, rehearsal and fallback, interruption and question handling, and an honest unknown-response. Actual assignment and recruiter instructions govern.
