# ML Design final implementation synthesis

Reviewed: 2026-09-04  
Decision: approved for the Required ML Design family.

## Published contract

- Exactly 20 substantive concept lessons and 13 distinct canonical dossiers.
- Eight roadmap stages map explicitly to concept and practice IDs.
- DECIDE is a flexible six-stage interview aid, not a rigid universal process.
- Guided, untimed, and 30/45/60-minute timed modes carry honest evidence labels.
- Public drafts remain tab-only. Explicit signed-in attempts are owner-scoped, revision-checked, private by default, and excluded from analytics.
- Self-review uses ten independent descriptive dimensions. No aggregate score, pass prediction, or readiness probability is calculated.
- Every concept and dossier records source IDs and a review date; sources carry usage limits in the ledger.
- Public routes are canonical, discoverable through navigation/search/sitemap, and covered by finite-route tests. Private attempt routes are non-indexable.

## Review record

The six synthesis artifacts were reconciled with `data/ml-design`, route inventory, source rendering, the private-attempt schema, and focused static/database contracts on 2026-09-04. All 16 cited records are primary papers, standards, official documentation, or first-party engineering/science sources.

## Guardrails

The implementation avoids employer provenance claims, universal model/storage recommendations, opaque scoring, and private-text analytics. Future expansion is a new requirement, not a condition for closing this Required family.
